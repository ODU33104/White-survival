# Stage 5 reference — bear / wave defense

Primary references:
- CreativeScope WS-S03
- CreativeScope WS-S08
- Google Play user reports describing polar-bear combat and meat collection.

## Loop

prepare food/resources -> upgrade wall/weapon pads -> bear or enemy wave appears -> fight in real space -> visible drops/rewards -> repair/upgrade -> stronger wave.

## Visual requirements

- Threat should enter the visible play area quickly.
- Bear/boss must be dramatically larger than player.
- Defensive objects must be visible physical structures, not HUD-only stats.
- Damage should visibly affect wall/structure condition.
- Kills should produce tangible rewards (meat/resources/coins) that can be carried or collected.
- Camera should keep player and immediate threat in the same readable frame.
